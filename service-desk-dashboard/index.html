How these numbers are worked out

Before = average of Nov 2025 to Jan 2026, the three months before the dashboard went live in Feb 2026. After = average of the latest three months. Hours saved per year = (reporting hours before − after) × 12, plus (review prep hours before − after) × reviews per year. Cost = hours × blended hourly cost. Client wait time avoided = incidents per month × drop in incident MTTR. Extra first contact fixes = human-raised tickets per month × rise in FCR rate. Every figure on this page is sample data built for this prototype.
